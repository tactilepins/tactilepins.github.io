Common questions

How do I press the pins?

You start on the back, looking at the pin tips. Push with a finger, a whole hand or an Apple Pencil; the longer you hold, the deeper the pins go. Wide and Thin switch between a whole fingertip and a touch that moves only a pin or two.

How do I turn the toy around?

Drag with two fingers, or with one finger anywhere that isn't the pins, and pinch to zoom. Turn chooses which ways a drag may turn it: left and right (the default, so it can't be spilled by accident), up and down, any way, or locked. Flip cycles through the back, an edge-on view and the front.

All my pins fell out.

Gravity is real. With Turn set to any way, tipping the toy glass-down lets the pins drop to the glass, and so can turning the device face-down or shaking it. Undo puts them back, and Reset tips the toy glass-up so every pin slides home.

How do I press a real toy, a hand or a face in — or capture it in color?

Pick Back cam or Front cam. While a camera is active, everything else tucks away and four options appear: Cancel (puts the pins back as they were), Keep (leaves the live pins as they are), Press in (finishes a clean 3D shape from a moment of frames), and In color (finishes the 3D shape and paints every pressed pin with the real colors the camera sees). Good light and a plain background help. In color is part of the full set.

Does the camera work on my device?

Yes. Devices with LiDAR, a pair of back cameras or a front TrueDepth camera measure real distance; every other iPhone, iPad and Mac computes depth with a model that runs on the device. On a Mac the built-in camera works as the front camera.

How do I 3D print a creation?

Save your creation, open it in the Gallery, and tap 3D. Choose Small (100 mm), Medium (160 mm) or Large (220 mm) by longest side, and Shallow or Deep relief, then send the STL file to your 3D printer's slicing app. Each pin becomes a six-sided column packed edge-to-edge on a flat 2 mm base plate so it prints as one solid piece. STL files hold the 3D shape only (not paint or pin colors). Exporting STL files is part of the full set.

How does paint fill work?

In Paint, switch Brush to Fill and tap a pin. The paint spreads until it meets another color or a step in the height of the pins, so a raised shape fills without spilling onto the pins around it. You can pick from 26 colors or choose any color with the color picker.

How do I keep what I made?

The shutter button saves where every pin is, with a photo. Open it in the Gallery to keep editing, pin it to the top, share the photo, export for 3D printing, or delete it. To put the picture in your photos, share it and choose Save Image.

How do I give a creation to someone?

In the Gallery, open a creation and tap Code. On the other iPhone, iPad or Mac, open the Gallery, then Scan a code, and point it at the code, or choose a picture of the code from your photos. A detailed or full-color creation that is too large for one QR code is shown automatically as a loop of codes; the scanner gathers the parts in any order so every pin and color arrives intact (a simpler single code is also offered for sending as a picture). Making a code is part of the full set; scanning one is always free.

What is in the full set?

The pins, the magnet, both cameras and Press in are free, with a starter box of stamps, paint colors, pins and backgrounds and three saves. The full set is one 99¢ ($0.99) purchase that opens all 39 stamps, 26 paint colors plus the custom color picker, custom metal and plastic pin colors, all 15 scenes and your own photo background, capturing camera shapes in color, unlimited saves, sharing by code, and STL files for 3D printing. It is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought the full set but it is locked.

Tap any padlock, or go to Style, then About, and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I use it on a Mac — and is Android coming?

On a Mac, click and drag to press, drag beside the pins or scroll to turn it, and pinch to zoom. Save is ⌘S, Gallery ⌘G, Reset ⌘R, Flip ⌘F and Undo ⌘Z. A Mac has no motion sensors, so there is no shake to clear: use Reset. An Android version is also coming soon on Google Play.

Why does it ask for the camera or my photos?

The camera is for pressing things into the pins (with or without color) and scanning codes; what it sees is processed on the device and never kept or sent. Photos is asked only to add a picture when you save one of a creation there. Everything else works if you say no, and you can change it later in your device's Settings. See the privacy policy.

Does it need the internet?

No. Tactile Pins works fully offline. Only buying or restoring the full set needs the App Store.
