Common questions

How do I press the pins?

You start on the back, looking at the pin tips. Push with a finger, a whole hand or an Apple Pencil; the longer you hold, the deeper the pins go. Wide and Thin switch between a whole fingertip and a touch that moves only a pin or two.

How do I turn the toy round?

Drag with two fingers, or with one finger anywhere that isn't the pins, and pinch to zoom. Turn chooses which ways a drag may turn it: left and right (the default, so it can't be spilled by accident), up and down, any way, or locked. Flip goes round the back, an edge-on view and the front.

All my pins fell out.

Gravity is real. With Turn set to any way, tipping the toy glass-down lets the pins drop to the glass, and so can turning the device face-down or shaking it. Undo puts them back, and Reset tips the toy glass-up so every pin slides home.

How do I press a real toy, a hand or a face in?

Pick Back cam or Front cam. With the back camera, point at a toy and move closer: the nearer you get, the further its shape goes in. With the front camera, bring a toy, a hand or your face toward the screen. When it looks right, hold still and tap Press in, and the app takes a careful version from a moment of frames. Good light and a plain background help.

Does the camera work on my device?

Yes. Devices with LiDAR, a pair of back cameras or a front TrueDepth camera measure real distance; every other iPhone, iPad and Mac works the depth out with a model that runs on the device. On a Mac the built-in camera works as the front camera.

How does paint fill work?

In Paint, switch Brush to Fill and tap a pin. The paint spreads until it meets another colour or a step in the height of the pins, so a raised shape fills without spilling on to the pins around it.

How do I keep what I made?

The shutter button saves where every pin is, with a photo. Open it in the Gallery to carry on, pin it to the top, share the photo or delete it. To put the picture in your photos, share it and choose Save Image.

How do I give a creation to someone?

In the Gallery, open a creation and tap Code. On the other iPhone, iPad or Mac, open the Gallery, then Scan a code, and point it at the code, or choose a picture of the code from your photos. It opens there ready to carry on with, with no internet or account. Making a code is part of the full set; scanning one is always free.

What is in the full set?

The pins, the magnet, both cameras and Press in are free, with a starter box of stamps, paint colours, pins and backgrounds and three saves. The full set is one purchase that opens every stamp, colour, pin finish and background, a photo of your own as the background, unlimited saves and sharing by code. It is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought the full set but it is locked.

Tap any padlock, or go to Style, then About, and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I use it on a Mac?

Click and drag to press. Drag beside the pins or scroll to turn it, and pinch to zoom. Save is ⌘S, Gallery ⌘G, Reset ⌘R, Flip ⌘F and Undo ⌘Z. A Mac has no motion sensors, so there is no shake to clear: use Reset.

Why does it ask for the camera or my photos?

The camera is for pressing things into the pins and scanning codes; what it sees is worked on on the device and never kept or sent. Photos is asked only to add a picture when you save one of a creation there. Everything else works if you say no, and you can change it later in your device's Settings. See the privacy policy.

Does it need the internet?

No. Tactile Pins works fully offline. Only buying or restoring the full set needs the App Store.
