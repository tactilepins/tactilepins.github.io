Privacy policy

Tactile Pins collects no data at all. Here are the details.

Effective November 21, 2026

Tactile Pins is a pin art toy for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is built to work entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal information, from anyone, because none of it ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or data-collecting code. Apart from the App Store purchase described below, the app makes no network requests.

The camera

The camera is used only while you choose to: when the front or back camera tool is on, to press something into the pins, and when you scan a creation's code. Depth comes from your device's own depth sensors (LiDAR, a pair of cameras, or the front TrueDepth camera) where it has them, and otherwise from a depth model that comes inside the app and runs on the device. The camera's pictures are worked on in the device's memory and then dropped. They are never saved, never sent anywhere and never seen by anyone. Only the shape they leave in the pins is kept, and only if you save it.

What you make

Saved creations (where every pin is, the paint and the finish, with a picture of the toy) are kept only in the app's private storage on your device, along with your settings.

Photos

If you choose a photo of your own to go behind the pins, a copy of that one photo is kept in the app's private storage, on the device, until you choose another or remove the app. If you read a creation's code from a picture, the picture is read on the device and not kept. Both use the system's photo picker, so the app sees only the photo you pick and not the rest of your library.

When you share the picture of a creation, you can choose Save Image in the share sheet to add it to your photos. The first time, you are asked for permission. The app can only add to your photos; it can't read them.

Sharing by code

A creation can be turned into a QR code. The creation itself (the pins, paint and finish) is written into the code, with nothing about you. It is shared only when you show it to someone or send the picture yourself, and is read by the other device with no internet and no server in between.

The full set

The app is free. One optional purchase, the full set, is bought through the App Store. The App Store handles the payment: the app only asks it for the price and whether the full set has been bought, which is the one time it uses the network. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.

Sound and haptics

The pin sounds are made by the app as you play, and the taps you feel come from your device. The app never uses the microphone.

Permissions

The camera, asked for the first time you use a camera tool or scan a code, and adding to your photos, asked the first time you save a picture of a creation to them. You can say no, and everything else works the same. You can change either later in your device's Settings. Tactile Pins doesn't use location, contacts, the microphone or your photo library beyond the photos you pick.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. This is true for children under 13 (COPPA), and in every country.

Deleting your data

Deleting a creation in the gallery removes it. Deleting Tactile Pins from your device removes all of its data. Pictures you saved to your photos stay there until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
